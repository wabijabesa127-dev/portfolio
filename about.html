Enrolled in Information Systems program to gain structured knowledge and formal education in technology.

University gave me a solid foundation in Information System concepts. I learned about system design, database management, and got exposure to various programming paradigms. It also helped me build a network of like-minded peers.

Started learning Java, Object-Oriented Programming, Data Structures, and Algorithms. Built several console applications.
